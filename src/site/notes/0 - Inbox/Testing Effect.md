# A tool to build mastery of any topic
In virtually all areas of learning, you build better mastery when you use testing as a tool to identify gaps and bring forth areas of weakness

---
We lose around 70% of what we've just heard or read.

Practicing retrieval makes learning stick far better than re-exposure to the original material does. This is the testing effect, AKA the retrieval-practice effect.

# The Testing Effect in the Lab
Researchers showed that simply asking a subject to fill in a word's missing letters resulted in better memory of the word.

This experience was a demonstration of what researchers call the [[1 - Projects/Reading Making It Stick/Generation effect\|Generation effect]]
The modest effort required to generate the cued answer while studying the pairs strengthened memory of the target word tested later.

This study found that the ability to recall the word pair on later tests was greater if the practice retrieval was delayed by twenty intervening word pairs than when it came immediately after first studying the pair.
