Increased familiarity with a piece of information and fluency when ingesting it can create the illusion that we have mastery over it.

In the case of a piece of text, the fact that one can repeat the contents from memory doesn't necessarily mean that a solid understanding of the contents has been achieved. That shows no indication that one understands the core principles, their applications and their links to other concepts.
